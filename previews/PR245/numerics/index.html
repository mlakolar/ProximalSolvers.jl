Numerical behavior

CD's private PB SparseIterate retains stored activation/compaction order. First full passes admit coordinates, active passes refine them, and only a full pass strictly below the change tolerance converges. Random LS uses historical momentum/restarts and refreshes moved residual caches before full checks; the borrowed RNG advances. Cyclic and explicit momentum=false remain supported. Zero columns choose the nearest-zero penalty minimizer, including lambda=0. Nonzero-column curvature underflow is an error. tol=0 stays strict; maxpasses=0 returns unconverged with no updates. A nonfinite returned objective is refused before dense scatter; this is not robust dynamic-range recovery.

PGD retains native LS image/value/gradient order, scalar steps, fused PB prox, TFOCS objective-to-gradient checks, finite curvature-growth refusal and zero-step gradient refresh. Historical objective/iterate/FASTA convergence tests combine by OR. Converged does not guarantee KKT below gradtol; objective rounding can stop even with very small ftol. Check objective/KKT independently. Native Float32 may stagnate earlier. Finite inputs do not ensure finite intermediates; historical LS image exceptional-range limitations remain outside this extraction.

Callbacks/display sample printevery; stored traces include each iteration. A callback receives the trace when storing, otherwise a state. Limits are checked after an iteration and counts may overshoot. iterationconverged means a limit, not success. Positive gaptol is refused; positive gapevery is inert at gaptol=0. The retained scalar APIs/results do not introduce a new stopping/logging engine.
