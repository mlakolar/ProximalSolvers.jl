Matrix proximal gradient for the graphical lasso

PGD-06 Matrix inputs and ownership

Statement. solve!(Θ, f, g, method::ProximalGradientDescent, options) solves the full-matrix objective in ADM-04 with ProximalBase f = LogDetLoss(S) and g = NormL1(λ₀, Λ). The start is a nonempty square Matrix{T} for T ∈ {Float32, Float64}. The stored S and Λ are Matrix{T} of that same size. The three matrices have finite entries and exact symmetry by entrywise equality. Θ must pass Cholesky in T. The loss weight must be one, Λ must be nonnegative with zero diagonal, and λ₀ must be finite, nonnegative and exactly representable in T. Each effective weight Cᵢⱼ = T(λ₀) Λᵢⱼ must be finite. Pairwise alias checks include separately wrapped arrays whose memory overlaps. The solver borrows the inputs for reads and assumes the caller leaves them unchanged while the solve runs.

The method requires BackTracking with Lexact = Inf, and the converted factors satisfy 0 < α ≤ 1 with finite 1/α and 0 < β < 1 in T. Accepted options are maxiter, ftol, xtol, gradtol, time_limit and the three oracle-call limits. Objective increases, tracing, callbacks and print cadence require their defaults. Additional solve keywords, unsupported functions or buffer types, and invalid data or parameters raise ArgumentError; mismatched dimensions and empty or nonsquare starts raise DimensionMismatch. Preflight and start diagnostics run before writes to the caller's matrix.

Applies to. Dense matrix PGD with the graphical-lasso function pair.

Code. src/solvers/logdet_proximal_gradient.jl and the shared array validation primitives in src/solvers/admm.jl.

Tests. test/pgd_graphical_lasso.jl exercises both precisions, invalid methods, options, dimensions, weights, storage, borrowed buffers and starts.

Sources. Approved graphical-lasso PGD scope.

PGD-07 SPD backtracking and rollback

Statement. At the current SPD point Θ, ProximalBase evaluates the logdet gradient G = S - inv(Θ). The solver requires finite entries. It replaces each pair of off-diagonal entries by their mean if their difference is at most 8eps(T) times the largest of one and the magnitudes of the two entries and the corresponding entry of S. Larger skew raises an error. The first search starts at t = 1. Subsequent searches start at min(1, t_last / T(α)).

The solver forms exactly symmetric V = Θ - t G in private storage and calls the ProximalBase prox!(Z, g, V, t) if V is finite. The proposal must be finite, exactly symmetric and pass Cholesky in T before the solver evaluates its smooth value. With D = Z - Θ, it requires both

\[f(Z) \le f(\Theta) + \langle G,D\rangle + \frac{\|D\|_F^2}{2t}, \qquad f(Z)+g(Z) \le f(\Theta)+g(\Theta)\]

with a working-precision allowance on each comparison. The allowance is 16eps(T) times the largest of one and the magnitudes of the two loss values, the linear term and the quadratic term in the first comparison. For the composite comparison it scales with the largest of one and the two composite magnitudes. The term-based scale allows for rounding in the oracle values and arithmetic near equality, including cancellation in the model. The two inner products run over the full matrix. A nonfinite objective or quadratic model fails the comparison.

Each rejection shrinks the step by T(β) if the solve has budget to continue. A search attempts at most 64 trials, with finite positive step and reciprocal required before each trial. Failure to reduce the step, an invalid step or 64 rejections raises an error. ProximalBase evaluates the candidate gradient after its SPD and acceptance checks. The solver commits the candidate only after the gradient and KKT diagnostics succeed. A thrown numerical error or an interrupted search leaves the last committed SPD point in the caller's matrix. The step depends on local comparisons because the logdet gradient has no global Lipschitz constant on the SPD cone.

Applies to. Matrix PGD after preflight in PGD-06.

Code. src/solvers/logdet_proximal_gradient.jl implements the matrix step within _solve_gradient! in src/solve.jl.

Tests. test/pgd_graphical_lasso.jl verifies an indefinite first trial, a nonfinite objective, finite backtracking and bounded failure, invalid reciprocal and rollback after a completed step.

Sources. Approved graphical-lasso PGD iteration.

PGD-08 KKT stopping, limits and results

Statement. At a committed point, the KKT score is the largest entrywise violation divided by max(1, maximum(abs, S), maximum(C)). The violations are abs(Gᵢᵢ) on the diagonal, abs(Gᵢⱼ + Cᵢⱼ sign(Θᵢⱼ)) at nonzero off-diagonal entries, and max(abs(Gᵢⱼ) - Cᵢⱼ, 0) at exact zeros. The scale, products and normalized score are evaluated in T. Nonfinite violations raise an error. After a completed step, convergence requires g_residual ≤ g_tol, where g_tol = max(gradtol, 64eps(T)). The check is independent of the trial step and the iterate and objective change tests. An accepted elementwise unchanged point outside the KKT threshold sets stagnated=true and converged=false.

OptimizationResults.minimizer aliases the caller's matrix. minimum is f(Θ) + g(Θ) there. x_residual is ‖Z - Θ_previous‖_F, and f_residual follows the relative objective change in RES-02. Both are NaN before the first completed step. x_converged and f_converged require convergence and their respective change to be below the tolerance. ftol=0 disables the objective proposal. grad_converged equals converged. f_increased records an actual increase on the last completed step, which can occur within the comparison allowance. The trace is empty and gap=NaN.

iterations counts completed steps, including an accepted unchanged point. backtrack_steps counts attempted proposals, including accepted ones and proposals rejected before the prox. f_calls counts value-only loss calls and the initial combined value/gradient oracle, grad_calls counts gradient-only calls and the same initial combined oracle, and prox_calls counts actual penalty proximal calls. Penalty value evaluations are outside the smooth loss count. The initial diagnostics consume one loss and one gradient call. A zero call limit is unlimited. The solver checks positive call limits and time before the search and after rejections and completed steps. A completed step may set a limit flag even when the retained point meets the KKT threshold. An exit by the iteration, time or call limit returns converged=false. iteration_converged records exhaustion of maxiter, and call_limit_reached records consumption of any positive call budget.

Applies to. Normal matrix PGD returns.

Code. src/solvers/logdet_proximal_gradient.jl supplies the KKT and rounding-stall checks. _solve_gradient! in src/solve.jl handles limit exits and constructs OptimizationResults.

Tests. test/pgd_graphical_lasso.jl independently checks SPD, primal objective, KKT and a feasible dual lower bound for analytic and seeded problems. It also tests reported counters, a verified fixed point, a rounded Float32 stall outside the threshold and limit precedence. A call limit during a rejected trial checks that the shared driver keeps the last accepted point and its diagnostics.

Sources. Approved graphical-lasso PGD stopping rule.
