Deferred and refused features

The current supported combinations are IN-01 and the specialized solver contracts. A retained constructor keyword is not evidence of implemented support.

DEF-01 Unsupported capabilities and compatibility sentinels

Statement.

The following remain deferred:

  • sparse design matrices; sparse vectors and views as coefficient iterates; SparseIterate for PGD/APG; other group and symmetric owner types;
  • matrix-shaped coefficient owners except the dense Matrix coefficient of least-squares CD with a matrix response and native row-group GroupNormL2 (CDM-01, #396) and the dense Matrix iterate of the graphical lasso with ADMM or PGD (ADM-04, PGD-06), and the matrix-response entrywise lasso with PGD or APG (IN-10); matrix-response regression penalties and storage outside those solver contracts, including weighted matrix NormL1;
  • Scaled penalties (including Scaled of a weighted ElasticNet, whose weighted form is specified in IN-03), and CD group penalties other than GroupNormL2;
  • SqrNormL2 as a CD penalty with a declared center type other than Nothing, including a zero-valued center or a union-typed center holding nothing. The uncentered scalar least-squares CD form is specified in IN-01. PGD/APG, square-root CD and group/matrix CD also exclude SqrNormL2 penalties;
  • weighted NormL1Nonneg, nonnegative least squares or nonnegative lasso with APG or with CD on a SparseIterate, BlockIterate or Matrix, and the other constrained-penalty combinations excluded by IN-07 (CD on a dense Vector supports the unweighted forms);
  • automatic intercept handling and offsets (a column of ones can be supplied as an unpenalized coordinate, with penalty weight zero, under the input rules of a weighted NormL1); generalized losses and compositions, except the raw LogisticLoss with NormL1 and ProximalGradientDescent or AccProxGradDescent in the scope of IN-09 and APG-08, with ProxNewton in the scope of PN-01, and with LogisticADMM in the scope of LA-01. For the logistic loss, CDSolver, ADMMSolver, Newton-type methods other than ProxNewton, ADMM variants other than LogisticADMM (adaptive ρ, over-relaxation, general linear constraints), Scaled(LogisticLoss), observation weights and offsets, Float32 data, sparse designs and penalties other than NormL1 remain deferred, as do Poisson and other generalized linear model losses;
  • monotone or active-set acceleration, APG diagonal preconditioning, Newton (other than ProxNewton for the raw logistic loss, PN-01), primal-dual methods, SLOPE and direct precision-matrix solvers other than the graphical-lasso routes in ADM-04 and PGD-06;
  • ADMM outside the centered quadratic and unweighted ℓ₁ problem in ADM-01, the least-squares lasso with a tall or square Float64 design in ADM-05, the graphical lasso in ADM-04 and the predictor split of sparse logistic regression in LA-01 (which accepts wide designs), including arbitrary function pairs, product spaces, linear constraints, custom norms, scaled or Float32 least-squares losses, wide designs (n < p) for the consensus least-squares ADMM of ADM-05, and over-relaxation or the adaptive penalty on a matrix;
  • paths, screening, public workspaces, refitting, reweighting, and certificates other than the square-root-lasso CD duality certificate;
  • generic block, metric, certificate and logging frameworks, and extension promises based on the private model/state hooks.

Nondefault requests for deferred features are refused. Some constructor keywords remain accepted only at their compatibility defaults, so historical signatures can give informative errors. The solver/options objects do not store those unavailable features. CD's kkt_checks = 0 is a compatibility sentinel. gap = NaN is also a placeholder unless square-root-lasso CD has certified its result and stores the bound; see RES-01.

This support reduction and the relation to historical branches are recorded in HIST-01. The private CD model hooks do not establish a public custom-loss or workspace API; see ARCH-03.

Applies to. Current development-core constructors, options and public solve dispatch.

Code. src/types.jl: constructor validation; src/solve.jl: supported dispatch and input gates; src/cd/models.jl: private seams.

Tests. test/core_contracts.jl, test/naming.jl, test/nonnegative_lasso.jl, test/cd_nonnegative.jl, test/logistic_pgd.jl (the logistic refusals), test/logistic_apg.jl and test/logistic_proxnewton.jl (cases under the shared gate for AccProxGradDescent and ProxNewton), test/logistic_admm.jl (the same refusals for LogisticADMM) and solver-specific unsupported-input tests.

Sources. docs/src/support.md:L385, docs/src/developer/implementation-notes.md:L238, docs/src/developer/implementation-notes.md:L240, docs/src/developer/implementation-notes.md:L434, docs/src/index.md:L9, README.md:L96; the later nonnegative-lasso refusals are specified by upstream #413 at fd45a42. The narrow ridge extension and remaining refusals are upstream #420 at 217cde72.
