Float32 PGD and APG stopping: measurements

This page records the measurements behind STOP-18, STOP-19 and STOP-20 (issue #320). They are observations at stated revisions, not solver guarantees.

evidence-float32-stopping-320 Study and provenance

Statement. The Phase 0 study of #320 ran Float32 PGD, with Float64 PGD and Float32 APG as controls, at ProximalSolvers 637f06a, 9f86a80 and dac9f4b on Julia 1.10.13 and 1.13.3 (Intel Xeon E5-2640 v4 and Xeon Silver 4116). The designs were the five harness designs lasso, lasso-B (cold and warm), lasso-C and lasso-D at λ = 0.15 λmax, and an adverse set of seven correlated, equicorrelated, ill-conditioned and wide designs, six of them at λ = 0.02 λmax. Each design had 10 draws. Independent gaps and KKT residuals were evaluated in Float64 on the rounded inputs; for the library-run matrix records they were also checked in 256-bit BigFloat. The scripts, preregistration, per-solve summaries and the report are on the branch archive/issue-320-evidence at fb8fb87d; the report is the Phase 0 comment.

  • Exits under the harness options. In the 240 Float32 PGD records at dac9f4b on the Xeon E5-2640 v4 (harness and adverse designs, both Julia versions), the objective test made the first stop proposal in 237 records; no proposal was made in the other 3. All 156 objective-increase exits were rises of one to three Float32 spacings of F, 142 of exactly one. The Xeon Silver 4116 rerun had 157 objective-increase exits. These exits form the STOP-18 objective-increase class; the flag records only the sign of the change, and the relative size is in f_residual.

  • Computed-KKT convergence and the gap. Under the harness options, on the Xeon E5-2640 v4 and across the three revisions, each of the 231 Float32 PGD records with converged = true had an independent normalized KKT residual below 64 eps(Float32) ≈ 7.63e-6. Among those that missed the 1e-5 target, the ratio of gap to normalized KKT residual was 2.3 to 7.0 on the harness designs and 16.9 to 47.2 on the adverse set. Dividing the STOP-13 bound by the normalized residual gives (2 + k) S/λ for a positive residual; its leading term 2S/λ is about 13 at λ = 0.15 λmax and 100 at λ = 0.02 λmax.

  • Numerical check of STOP-13 on the archive. The archive's per-solve summaries hold 25,200 library-run rows with finite 256-bit gap and normalized KKT residual (gap_big, kkt_big; all precisions, solvers, option profiles, arms and CPUs; rows of the same solve on several arms are counted separately). The other rows (540 library-run rows and the trajectory-derived rows of the simulated gradient-tolerance sweep) have no 256-bit values and are excluded. The residual is normalized by an independently recomputed scale, not by the solver's working-precision S. With k taken as that residual divided by the design's nominal λ / λmax, no row's gap exceeded 2k + k². The largest ratio of gap to that bound was 0.67 for Float32 PGD with converged = true (2,119 rows), 0.65 for Float32 APG with converged = true (4,830 rows) and 0.55 for Float64 PGD.

  • Rows with independent residual below τ. The residual hypothesis of STOP-19 uses the solver's computed S, which the archive does not store, so it cannot be checked exactly. With the independent scale, 14 converged Float32 APG rows had kkt_big above the profile's τ, by at most a factor 1.00024. They are copies, across revisions and CPUs, of two solves: adv-equi-low-cold draw 5 with the library defaults on Julia 1.13.3 reported convergence at 1.00024e-5 against τ = 1e-5, and adv-ill-low-cold draw 6 with gradtol = 1e-7 on Julia 1.10.13 at 7.6297e-6 against the floor 7.6294e-6. On the converged rows with kkt_big ≤ τ (2,119 Float32 PGD, 4,816 Float32 APG and 5,906 Float64 PGD), no gap exceeded the ceiling B of STOP-19, computed from τ and the nominal fraction; the largest ratio was 0.62.

  • The checks, run with python3 -I on the extracted archive:

    import gzip, csv, glob, sys, math
frac = {"adv-cor-low-cold": .02, "adv-cor-low-warm": .02, "adv-equi-low-cold": .02, "adv-ill-mid-cold": .15,
        "adv-ill-low-cold": .02, "adv-ill-low-warm": .02, "adv-wide-low-cold": .02}   # harness designs: 0.15
gradtol = {"D": 1e-5, "H": 1e-11, "S": 1e-11, "A": 1e-10}          # profile Q: 1e-7 (Float32), 1e-10 (Float64)
eps = {"Float32": 2.0**-23, "Float64": 2.0**-52}
for fn in glob.glob(sys.argv[1] + "/results/*/*/run-summary.tsv.gz"):
    for r in csv.DictReader(gzip.open(fn, "rt"), delimiter="\t"):
        if "lib" not in r["kind"]: continue
        g, kr = float(r["gap_big"] or "nan"), float(r["kkt_big"] or "nan")
        if not (math.isfinite(g) and math.isfinite(kr)): continue
        f = frac.get(r["design"], .15); k = kr / f
        assert g <= 2 * k + k * k                                  # STOP-13 with measured k
        if r["converged"] == "true":
            gt = gradtol.get(r["profile"], 1e-7 if r["T"] == "Float32" else 1e-10)
            tau = max(gt, 64 * eps[r["T"]]); a = tau / f
            assert kr > tau or g <= 2 * a + a * a                  # ceiling B on rows with kkt_big <= tau

  • Exit fields. On 1a9f196b with Julia 1.13.4, a Float32 lasso solve stopped by a callback and one stopped by time_limit = 0 returned the same flags for both PGD and APG: converged, f_increased, stagnated, iteration_converged and call_limit_reached were all false after one iteration (STOP-18).

Applies to. The recorded Phase 0 populations and revisions; the exit-field probe at 1a9f196b.

Code. Solver source at the recorded revisions; the analysis scripts on the archive branch.

Tests. The counts are measurements, not contract accuracy targets.

Sources. Issue #320, the Phase 0 report and archive/issue-320-evidence at fb8fb87d.
