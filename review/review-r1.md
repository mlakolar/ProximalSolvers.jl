1. **BLOCKER — S can accept a grossly non-stationary point while violating its stated resolution condition.** Report: §§1 and 7, `report/findings.md:18–20,183–187,195`. In `prototypes/S/S1024.diff:42–52,165–174`, both initialization's group norm and the stop-time bound form unscaled sums of `v*v`. These can underflow to zero even though the coefficients, column products, curvature, response scale, and the required resolution estimate are representable. The fast return then bypasses the actual norm calculation and returns state 0.

   A cluster probe on the exact archived patch confirms this in both precisions. In Float64, take `X = fill(2.0^300, 1, 2)`, `y = [2.0^-360]`, one group `[1,2]` with unit weight and zero penalty, and start `[2.0^-600, -2.0^-600]`. The exact KKT residual divided by the zero-point gradient norm is **1**, the true loss is **9.065110999561118e-218**, and the report's `ρ(x)/L` is **5.12e9**. The block curvature version of ρ also exceeds L. The Float32 analogue gives **2.097152e6**. Evidence: `../review-probes/probe-r2.jl:21–36` and `../review-probes/probe-r2.log:34–37`; loaded source `../proto3/S1024/src/cd/leastsquares.jl:742–751`. Job **14340095**, Julia 1.13.4, through the exact cluster command supplied in the request.

   This is underflow in S's newly introduced check, not underflow or cancellation of the coordinate correlation products excluded as F1/F3. 3. **BLOCKER — The contract promotes a resolution proxy into a numerical guarantee that it does not establish.** Report: `report/findings.md:5,13,18,186–187,197,202`. `eps(T)·Σ‖X_k‖|x_k|` is neither a lower bound on unavoidable residual error nor, with constant 1, an upper bound on the initialization error.    Conversely, consider the package's actual initialization order (`src/cd/leastsquares.jl:27–34`), with `T=Float32`, `y=[1]`, 8192 columns equal to 1, and each coefficient `eps(T)/4`. This is an exact binary calculation; no underflow, nonfinite inputs, or cancelled correlation products are involved. Forming the image first gives a different, accurate result at this same low-ρ point. Sequential accumulation has dimension-dependent error, and subtracting from y introduces an error term missing from ρ. The assertions about resolving L and excluding points where summation order matters do not follow from the proxy inequalities.

   **Correction:** state the contract in terms of the **computed tests actually passed**, and call ρ a heuristic amplification/resolution proxy. Remove the guarantees about actual residual accuracy and impossibility of resolving the tolerance. Likewise describe the observed association with initial error as empirical evidence, not a universal error-propagation theorem. A rigorous certificate is outside this issue's limits and is not required to make an honest, narrower statement.
