Independent acceptance targets used throughout: the repository's `CORE_TARGETS`, a relative suboptimality of `1e-8` (Float64) and `1e-5` (Float32); the square-root lasso against its own `1e-4`. A point is called within the target when its upper bound, against a reference whose own duality gap is at most `1e-12`, is below it. It is called above the target when a lower bound, its objective against that of a reference point, exceeds it; that needs no tight reference, only a reference objective that is positive and above `1e-20 F(0)` (an analysis cutoff that leaves out problems whose minimum is essentially 0). Both are 600-bit evaluations, not interval enclosures.

## 2. Decision trees

### Coordinate descent ([`src/cd/solver.jl`](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl))

- Threshold, once per solve ([`_tolerances`, L301 to L306](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L301-L306)): `relative && response_scale > 0` gives `tol · √(s Σy²)` and scaled changes; otherwise the absolute `tol`. A response scale that is not a normal number is refused before the first pass ([`response_scale`, L197 to L213](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L197-L213)).
- Loop ([`_cd_plain!`, L325 to L347](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L325-L347); with momentum [`_cd_momentum!`, L367 to L402](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L367-L402)):
  - The first pass is full. After a pass with `maxh < tol` the next pass is full; otherwise it is an active pass, except that a full pass is forced once the active passes since the last full one have made `20 p` visits ([L324 to L331](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L324-L331)).
  - A change is `abs(h) · √(s a_k)` when scaled ([`_visit!`, `src/cd/models.jl` L33 to L38](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/models.jl#L33-L38)); a non-finite change throws, and the caller's iterate is unchanged.
  - Only `full && maxh < tol` reaches the model's `_stop_check!`. Least squares and the group lasso return the constant `:converged` ([`models.jl` L20](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/models.jl#L20)): **no criterion can veto the stop**.
  - A zero-change full pass converges (`0 < tol`), unless `tol` or the effective threshold is 0, in which case the test cannot hold and the run ends at `maxpasses`.
  - With momentum, the extrapolation is applied before active passes only; a full pass starts from a recomputed residual when momentum moved the cache ([L375 to L379](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L375-L379)). The stopping pass is therefore a plain pass.
  - `maxpasses` exhausted: `converged = false` and a warning ([L493 to L500](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L493-L500)). The last pass may have been an active one.
- Result ([`_run_cd!`, L505 to L515](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/solver.jl#L505-L515)): `minimum` is the loss at the **cached** residual plus the penalty; a non-finite value throws.
- Scalar step ([`coordinate_step!`, `src/cd/leastsquares.jl` L96 to L108](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L96-L108)): `b = rᵀX_k` is formed in the data type before the scale ([`_column_dot`, L38 to L52](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L38-L52)); the range handling is entered on the scaled curvature only ([`_scaled_prox`, L109 to L114](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L109-L114)). This is where F1 and F3 arise; the residual initialization ([`_initialize!`, L27 to L37](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L27-L37)) and the rounded step ([L100 to L104](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L100-L104)) are where F2 arises.
- Group step ([L576 to L635](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/leastsquares.jl#L576-L635)): a zero block is first screened by the prox of the correlations; otherwise the majorization step with the curvature bound `ℓ_k`.
- Square-root stop check ([`_stop_check!`, `src/cd/sqrtlasso.jl` L302 to L319](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/sqrtlasso.jl#L302-L319)):
  - `_certify!` ([L441 to L526](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/sqrtlasso.jl#L441-L526)) recomputes the residual in Float64 and refreshes the cache. Certified: `converged = true`, `gap` = the bound rounded up.
  - `:failed`, the pass changed something, fewer than 2 tightenings, and `tol/100` is finite, positive and above the floor ([`_above_floor`, L321 to L328](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/cd/sqrtlasso.jl#L321-L328)): the loop continues with `tol/100`.
  - Otherwise the run stops uncertified: the certificate is undefined (a zero recomputed residual with a nonzero coefficient, a sum outside the range of the proof), the pass changed nothing, two tightenings were spent, or the floor forbids another.

### PGD and APG ([`_solve_gradient!`, `src/solve.jl` L207 to L284](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/solve.jl#L207-L284))

- Each iteration: `update_state!`, then a finite-objective check; an error restores the last accepted iterate and is rethrown ([L224 to L232](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/solve.jl#L224-L232)).
- `convergence_assessment` ([`src/utils.jl` L33 to L79](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L33-L79)), on the accepted iterate:
  - `stalled`: `ftol = 0` and the iterate equals the previous one ([L48](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L48)). A stalled iteration proposes nothing (F4).
  - `x` test: `‖x − x'‖ < xtol · max(‖x‖, 1)` ([L50](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L50)).
  - objective test, only for `ftol > 0`: relative change below `ftol`, or a change within about two units in the last place ([L57 to L61](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L57-L61)).
  - gradient test: relative to the largest residual so far or to the gradient scale, each only above the rounding resolution `ρ` ([`_gradient_test`, L94 to L99](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L94-L99)). PGD's residual is at the accepted iterate; APG's is `L ‖x − y‖∞`.
  - If any test holds, `_stationary!` ([L122 to L133](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L122-L133)) decides: `S = max(λ, λmax)` finite, `τ S ≥ floatmin(T)`, the residual finite and below `τ S`. If it refuses, all three flags are `false` and the solve continues.
  - PGD reads the gradient it holds ([L150](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L150)); APG forms it from the cached image ([L156 to L160](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L156-L160)); `λmax` is formed once ([L141 to L147](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/utils.jl#L141-L147)).
- Other exits, none of which sets `converged` ([L242 to L255](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/solve.jl#L242-L255)): an objective increase (unless `allow_f_increases`), stagnation ([`_stagnated`, `proximal_gradient_descent.jl` L327 to L329](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/solvers/proximal_gradient_descent.jl#L327-L329): Float32 PGD, `ftol = 0`, an unchanged iterate after a rejection decided by the computed curvature), a callback, the time limit, a call limit, `maxiter`.
- Trial and accepted iterates: the tests run after the line search has accepted a point (PGD [`backtrack!`, `src/line_searches.jl` L92 to L170](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/line_searches.jl#L92-L170); APG [`_prox_grad_step!`, L217 to L264](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/blob/b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89/src/solvers/accelerated_proximal_gradient_descent.jl#L217-L264)). A zero step is accepted by both.
- Result flags are those of the last iteration; `iteration_converged` is `iterations == maxiter`.

## 3. Provenance

- ProximalSolvers master `b8a2c04205f6dc8aadeecc42f4971602ab1b5b89`, clean detached worktree; the prototype X1 in a second worktree at the same commit plus `study/x1_stall_proposes.diff`.
- ProximalBase from `[sources]`: rev `a19c59aa723d085d5d7cf5242895bda16eb77214`, loaded tree hash `138929aa27c6221284ee1763e66b1110dd6ec418` (printed by the loading process on both Julia versions).
- Julia 1.13.4 and 1.10.13 (official Linux x86-64 builds), one Julia thread, OpenBLAS with one thread, local machine (Intel Raptor Lake; Julia 1.10 compiles for a CPU target without AVX there, 1.13 with it), shared with other work.
- The repository's benchmark harness was not run; its targets (`benchmark/cases.jl`, `CORE_TARGETS`) are reused.
- Inputs: 11 designs from a splitmix64 generator written in the scripts (no library random numbers, so the inputs are the same on both versions; the two sweeps were checked to have identical input columns). Orthogonal, i.i.d., equicorrelated 0.95, rank deficient with exact and near copies, underdetermined with noise and with a noiseless response, a zero column, column norms over six decades, tall with a noiseless response (after rounding, a near-exact fit: the response is within rounding of the column span, not in it), response with a `1e6` offset, `y = 0`. Sizes 20 to 60 rows, 8 to 60 columns. Each is rounded to the working type; every measure uses the rounded values.
- Penalty levels as fractions of the level at which the solution is 0: 0, 0.001, 0.1, 0.5, 1.01, 2 (lasso); the elastic net at 0, 0.1, 1.01 with `λ2 = 0.5 λmax`; the group lasso with groups of three and of sizes 1, 2, 3 and the rest; the square-root lasso at 0.001, 0.05, 0.3, 0.7, 1.01, 2. Raw and `1/n`-scaled loss. Starts: zero, the reference rounded to the type, the reference times 1.05 plus 0.001.
- CD settings: dense and `SparseIterate`; cyclic, random with the default momentum, random without momentum (seeds 1 and 2, `Xoshiro`, whose stream can differ between Julia versions); `tol` default, `1e-4`, and `1e-10` (Float64) or `1e-5` (Float32); `relative` true and, at the default `tol`, false; `maxpasses = 100000`.
- PGD and APG options: default; default with `maxiter = 20000`; that with `allow_f_increases`; with `ftol = 0`; with `gradtol = 1e-8`; the documented strict configuration. The trace was stored in every solve to find the first iteration within the target.
- Measures, all in 600-bit BigFloat with ordinary rounding (not interval arithmetic), stored in the tables to six significant digits: the minimal-norm KKT residual, the objective, and a dual value at the scaled residual (a lower bound of the minimum by weak duality). The reference of a problem is the best point found (the package's Float64 CD at `tol = 1e-15`, polished by a solve on its support; for `λ = 0` the least-squares minimizer computed in BigFloat), judged by its own BigFloat gap. The origin of a reference point does not matter, because its bounds are evaluated independently.
- Square-root lasso: the independent dual point is the 600-bit residual scaled to the boundary of the feasible set. It is not the production dual point (the Float64 residual with a conservative scaling). The comparisons of the reported `gap` with its gap and with the lower bound of the suboptimality are strict comparisons of the stored values; none fails. The 160 certified solves with `y = 0` return `x = 0` with `gap = 0`.
- Cross-version agreement of `converged`, solve by solve: all Float64 CD and square-root solves; 22,576 of 22,800 Float32 scalar CD, 4,493 of 4,512 Float32 group CD and 7,240 of 7,320 Float32 square-root solves; 6,819 of 6,840 Float64 and 6,406 of 6,840 Float32 PGD/APG solves. The Float32 gradient solvers flip mostly between `converged` and the objective-increase exit (F6).
