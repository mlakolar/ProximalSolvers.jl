## Evidence from the ProximalSolvers helper audit (PS #353): recommendation to defer `ProximalBase.NumericalKernels`

The Stage 0 audit of numerical helper ownership between ProximalSolvers and ProximalBase is posted on [ProximalSolvers #353](https://github.com/mlakolar/ProximalSolvers.jl/issues/353) ({PS_LINK}; section E is the interface part). It was research only and changed nothing in either package. This comment records what it found for the interface question of this issue. It proposes no implementation.

**Audited:** PB pin `a19c59aa` (what PS loads), PB master `13891ccf` for comparison, PS `dac9f4b`.

**Recommendation: defer the submodule; keep PB's kernels private and PS's certificate arithmetic in PS** (the first of the three options compared above). A separate package is not warranted: no consumer exists outside PB and PS.

Findings behind it:

1. **Size and shape.** `src/numerical_kernels.jl` has 778 executable lines, plus about 60 numerical lines in `src/utils.jl` (about 15 % of `src`). After #865 nearly every private primitive has two or more callers inside PB. None is exported; the guarantees are stated in comments and in the per-function pages, not as contracts of the primitives.
2. **PS already uses PB's supported API and nothing else.** It imports exported names and the four qualified-public oracle names (`leastsquares_data`, `value_from_residual`, `value_from_predictor`, `value_and_gradient_from_predictor!`). It reaches no private name.
3. **Real PB↔PS duplicates are three one-liners or short loops, 15 PS lines:** `Blocks._maxabs_diff` ≡ public `norm_diff(x, y, Inf)` (PS can delete it today, no PB change), `_sumsq_diff` ≡ private `_sumabs2_diff` (bitwise), `_clean_sum` against private `_squares_normal` (equal on finite inputs).
4. **The rest is "same family, different guarantee".** PB's kernels return stable values of `T`. PS's APG acceptance test needs an extended-range scalar with add, clipped subtract and exact compare (`_SE`), and its two certificates need one-sided bounds. PB has neither: no directed rounding, no extended-range type, no certified norm or spectral bound (`opnorm2` is documented as not certified). Every stable norm measured, including `_residual_squares` and `norm_diff`, is below the exact norm in 43–59 % of trials. `_scaled_product`/`_parts_product` agree with PS's `_se_mul` bit for bit in range, but return `Inf` where `_SE` returns `(1.0, 1800)`.
5. **What a submodule could take over:** at most 30–40 PS executable lines (`_se_norm` and its scaled sums through `_residual_squares`, about 20 net, in a cold APG fallback; `_sumsq_diff`; `_clean_sum`), against a supported namespace, docstrings with stated error theorems, coverage rows and a semver commitment at `0.6.0-DEV`, and with PS's proofs then resting on a PB kernel. That moves code and adds a layer.

**Thresholds for reopening** (all four): at least 50 net PS executable lines would migrate; PB states an accuracy theorem for the exposed kernel and the PS proofs are rewritten to cite it; paired Float32/Float64 timings on Julia 1.10 and the current release show the hot paths unchanged; and, for a separate package only, a third consumer independent of PB and PS.

**If the question returns:** PB's existing mechanism for a supported non-exported name (`Expr(:public, …)` under `VERSION >= v"1.11"` at `src/ProximalBase.jl:27`, importable by name on 1.10, enforced by `docs/check_api.jl`) is enough; a submodule adds only a namespace and either touches about 100 call sites in 11 files or leaves an alias layer. The contract an extended-range type would need in order to replace `_SE` is listed in section E of the PS report (normalized `s·2^e` with an `Int` exponent; product, sum and `max(a − b, 0)` each as one round-to-nearest significand operation; exact total comparison; stated behaviour at exponent gaps and under the Julia 1.10 `ldexp` truncation of #896; one-rounding construction; norm constructors with a stated error; zero allocation with fixed arities). PB meets the product clause today.

**Two PB observations that need no interface** (housekeeping, not requests): `_scaled_dot` (`src/numerical_kernels.jl:252`, with its `iterate_storage.jl` method) has no caller in `src` on the pin or on master; and the exported `shrink` (max/min form) takes 4.9 ns in a dependent scalar chain on Julia 1.10 against 4.3 ns for the private select form `_shrink_nonneg`, which matters only to downstream scalar callers such as a coordinate step. Separately, PS reads the fields `λ0`, `w`, `λ1`, `λ2`, `groups` of the exported penalty types, for which PB has no accessor.
