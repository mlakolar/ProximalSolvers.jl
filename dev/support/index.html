Supported API, ownership and migration

Vector y and Matrix X with the same Float32 or Float64 element type are supported, with positive consistent dimensions and finite entries. The iterate is a dense Vector of that type for both solvers, and additionally a one-dimensional SparseIterate of that type for CDSolver only; ProximalGradientDescent refuses every SparseIterate. Use raw PB LS or one positive finite scalar Scaled LS, and unweighted finite nonnegative NormL1. Parameters must be representable; positive values must not round to zero. Scaling is explicit: raw LS means sum(abs2,y-X*x)/2; divide by n explicitly for normalized lasso. Accepted wider scalars retain historical conversion sites.

X/y are borrowed read-only during solves and must not alias mutable x; for a SparseIterate the check uses the owner's whole backing storage, so even an owner with no active entries is refused when parent(activevalues(x)) or a reshape of it is passed as y or X. Results share x; extended traces copy x/gradient. Unsupported requests fail before mutation. A CD numerical exception preserves input x; PGD numerical step exceptions restore its last accepted point and rethrow. Counters/buffers are not generally transactional. Shape errors are DimensionMismatch; unsupported combinations/options are informative ArgumentError.

CD on a SparseIterate

  • Only the public active values are checked for finiteness. Every validation failure (unsupported owner, element type, shape, active NaN/Inf, data, loss, penalty, scalar, option, keyword or alias) happens before the owner is copied and before any RNG draw, and leaves the owner untouched.
  • The run uses work = copy(x), which keeps the active zeros and the slot order, and nothing is rebuilt through a dense vector. Passes, momentum and stopping are those of the dense wrapper; the extra storage is O(p).
  • A run that completes with a finite objective commits the complete final state with a same-shape copyto!(x, work) and returns x itself as minimizer. This covers a finite maxpasses limit (converged = false, with a warning) and maxpasses = 0, which leaves exactly the entry state, including active zeros, signed zeros and order. A normal pass compacts zero coordinates, and they are not restored; the order is not sorted.
  • A numerical error, such as a nonfinite step, a curvature that underflows or a nonfinite final objective, leaves the values, active set, signed zeros and order of x unchanged. The borrowed RNG may already have advanced; no rollback of the RNG is promised. Process termination, out-of-memory conditions and concurrent mutation of x, X or y are not covered.
  • After a successful call take activevalues(x) and activecoordinates(x) again: the active list may have been replaced or reordered.

Deferred

Sparse designs, sparse vectors and views as iterates, SparseIterate for proximal gradient descent, matrix-shaped, group and symmetric owners, weighted/group penalties, matrix responses, intercepts/offsets, generalized losses/compositions, acceleration, ADMM/Newton, primal-dual/SLOPE/graphical models, paths/screening/certificates/workspaces/refit and reweighting are deferred. Nondefault feature requests are refused. gap=NaN and CD kkt_checks=0 are compatibility sentinels, not computed diagnostics.

This is a breaking support reduction. Version/registration and any public release remain a separate decision; existing releases are untouched. Protected archive refs preserve the complete snapshot; legacy/full remains the historical working branch. Restore capabilities selectively; do not merge it wholesale.
