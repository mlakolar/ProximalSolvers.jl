Entry stopping thresholds: measurements

This page records the measurements behind the entry thresholds of STOP-03 and STOP-05 (issue #464). They are observations at stated revisions, not solver guarantees.

evidence-entry-thresholds-464 Study and provenance

Statement. Measured at ProximalSolvers 083c2f70 with ProximalBase e03c6d96, on Julia 1.13.4 and 1.10.13 (AMD EPYC 7513). Four rules for a proposed stop with NormL1(λ0, w) were compared:

  • the earlier single threshold τ max(maxᵢ cᵢ, λmax);
  • per-entry thresholds τ max(cᵢ, λmax), the rule adopted;
  • the single threshold plus a separate test of the zero-weight entries against τ λmax;
  • the single threshold with minᵢ cᵢ in place of maxᵢ cᵢ.

Accuracy was computed in 256-bit BigFloat from the stored data and the returned coefficients, against analytic minimizers or BigFloat coordinate-descent references. The scripts, raw tables and prototypes are on the branch research/issue-464-evidence at 28ba4e1f; the report is the #464 comment.

  • The #464 fixtures. Level λ0 = 1e300, weight 1 on an all-zero column (coefficient 1e300) and weight 0 on the free columns, default options, start at zero. Iterations / relative objective excess, single threshold → entry thresholds:

    CaseSingle thresholdEntry thresholds
    logistic PGD1 / 5.8e-36 / 2.4e-11
    least-squares PGD, one free column1 / 2.6e-321 / 2.6e-32
    least-squares PGD, two free columns1 / 2.28195 / 6.4e-8
    least-squares APG, one free column1 / 0.937 / 5.8e-11
    least-squares APG, two free columns1 / 0.5345 / 3.9e-8

    Every row reported converged = true. In the logistic row the minimizer has the predictor log 4; the single threshold stopped at the predictor 1.2, and the entry thresholds at 1.386282…, about 1.2e-5 from log 4.

  • Study of 204 configurations (PGD, APG, Float32, Float64; zero and excluded columns with weights up to 1e100, free and tiny weights, correlation 0.95, rescaling by 1e±100, logistic with a free intercept), Julia 1.13.4:

    • the single threshold reported 12 converged solves with excess above 1e-3 and 61 above the accuracy target (1e-8 in Float64, 1e-5 in Float32);
    • the entry thresholds reported 4 and 11, the 4 being the offset fixture of STOP-12 (#487);
    • the separate test of zero-weight entries missed tiny non-zero weights.

  • Minimum-coefficient rule. It decided as the entry thresholds in all those solves, but it can reject near-optimal points with an active coefficient far above λmax. Take X = [2.5 1e6; 0 1], y = [1, -1e6], NormL1(999999.27, [0, 1]) and maxiter = 2000. Its minimizer has the active x₂ = -0.73, and λmax = 2.5.

    • PGD from [292000.4, -0.73] reaches, at its second iterate [292000.4, -0.7299999999906868], a point with relative excess 8.7e-23. There the active entry's computed score is 9.31, against the entry threshold τ c₂ ≈ 10. The minimum-coefficient rule compares it with τ λmax = 2.5e-5 and rejects the point; PGD then took 27 iterations instead of 2.
    • APG from the minimizer of the stored problem, x₂ = big(999999.27) - big(1e6) and x₁ = (1 - 1e6 x₂)/2.5 rounded to [292000.39999254944, -0.7299999999813735] and scaled entrywise by 1 .+ [1e-9, -1e-6], ended after 7 iterations by an objective increase. It reported converged = false at excess 4e-25 under that rule, and converged = true under the entry thresholds. (Perturbing the displayed [292000.4, -0.73] instead does not separate the rules.)
    • Both Julia versions gave identical output (research464/scripts at the evidence revision, and the #464 thread).

  • Offset response (STOP-12, #487).

    • #464 study, offset case, Julia 1.13.4: X = [1 | Gaussian], n = 50, p = 10, y = 1e6 + signal + noise, zero weight on the intercept and one predictor, λ0 = 0.5 λ0max computed after fitting the free columns, default options. Every rule reported converged = true with relative excess 3.4 (PGD) and 46 (APG). The same response without the added 1e6 gave 8.8e-12 (PGD) and 5.1e-11 (APG, entry thresholds) in Float64. On Julia 1.10.13, whose seeded fixtures differ, the offset excesses were 6.1 and 14.
    • #400 validation, offset fixture: excess 0.22 at default options and 1.7e-7 with gradtol = 1e-8 (PGD).

  • Changed outcomes. When a coefficient exceeds λmax, Float32 solves that the single threshold stopped early can instead end by the objective-increase exit with converged = false, at more accurate points, as the problem without the excluded column does. With a zero weight and y = 0, no stop passes (STOP-03).

  • Group penalty. With singleton groups on the #464 fixture, APG kept a stop after one iteration at excess 0.93. The per-group rule stops after 7 iterations at 5.8e-11.

Applies to. Weighted least-squares NormL1 with PGD and APG; raw LogisticLoss with weighted NormL1 and PGD (IN-09); least-squares GroupNormL2 with PGD and APG.
