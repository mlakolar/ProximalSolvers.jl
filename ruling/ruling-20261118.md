## Maintainer ruling on the Stage 0 audit (recorded by the orchestrator)

The maintainer gave this ruling in the working session on 2026-11-18, on the summary of report revision 2 (the findings F1 to F9 and decisions D1 to D4 of the report that follows). It is quoted verbatim; link icons of the chat interface are dropped.

> I agree with most of the recommendations, but I would make **F2 a higher-priority follow-up** and **not approve the `ftol = 0` prototype yet**.
>
> The review has uncovered a real false-convergence problem, but the evidence does not justify redesigning the stopping machinery. We should preserve the current solver contracts, document their limitations, and investigate F2 separately.
>
> ## Maintainer ruling for ProximalSolvers #352
>
> | Finding | Decision |
> | --- | --- |
> | **F1/F3: Underflow and cancellation** | Approve documentation only |
> | **F2: CD empty-pass false convergence** | Document and open a focused, high-priority research issue |
> | **F4: `ftol = 0` stall** | Do not implement the current prototype |
> | **Documentation PR** | Approve one consolidated PR, coordinated with #320 |
>
> ### Detailed instructions
>
> **1. F1 and F3: APPROVED**
>
> Document the numerical limitations for CD, PGD and APG. Explain the distinction between meeting the implemented stopping criterion and possessing an independently certified optimum.
>
> Include the cancellation example with relative KKT residual 0.875, while making clear that its objective error is negligible.
>
> Do not add extended-precision recovery, modify default tolerances, or introduce new stopping machinery.
>
> **2. F2: DOCUMENT NOW, INVESTIGATE SEPARATELY**
>
> An objective value 7.4×10^16 times the minimum with `converged = true` is significant, even if it requires an extreme warm start.
>
> Open a narrowly scoped research issue examining reliable zero-change CD termination. Specifically:
>
> - Investigate inexpensive diagnostics at an empty full pass, including precision-aware stationarity checks.
> - Preserve legitimate Float32 convergence. A check that rejects 91 of 480 otherwise good solves is not acceptable unchanged.
> - Measure false positives, false negatives, extra products and ordinary-path performance.
> - Do not introduce a generic certification framework or numerical-recovery layer.
>
> This issue should not block closing #352 after its audit deliverables are complete, but it should remain tracked.
>
> **3. F4: NO-GO ON THE CURRENT PROTOTYPE**
>
> The prototype changes the documented behavior without resolving F1/F3 and can cause APG to exhaust `grad_calls_limit` earlier.
>
> Preserve the current implementation for now. Record the prototype and its measured benefits and costs.
>
> Reconsider a minimal fix only if it provides a demonstrable termination improvement without unacceptable extra gradient evaluations, changes to call-budget behavior, or new misleading successes. Submit that alternative for a separate approval rather than opening an implementation PR now.
>
> **4. Documentation PR: APPROVED**
>
> Produce one consolidated documentation PR after the audit is accepted.
>
> Coordinate with #320 and the already merged #327 documentation. Avoid duplicating existing warnings.
>
> Cover scalar CD, group CD, PGD, APG, stopping semantics, Float32 limitations, extreme warm starts, and `ftol = 0`. Report group CD's corrected 48/3,529 count and worst observed excess of 4.6×10^-5, explicitly distinguishing the benchmark target from the documented contract.
>
> ### Completion sequence
>
> 1. Finish independent review round 2 and address all justified findings.
> 2. Archive the reproducible evidence on `archive/issue-352-evidence`.
> 3. Post the final two-part report on #352, distinguishing high-precision evaluations from rigorous proofs.
> 4. Open the focused F2 research issue and cross-link it.
> 5. Prepare the consolidated documentation PR. Obtain independent Codex review and green final-head CI.
> 6. Leave all merges to the maintainer.
>
> No production solver changes are authorized by this ruling. This also preserves #352's original research-only scope.
>
> **Bottom line:** F2 deserves more investigation than F4. I would prioritize preventing genuinely bad false convergence over making the `ftol = 0` stopping behavior more convenient.

Consequences for the report: its recommendations are left as written, and its decision table carries this ruling next to each. D3 (the prototype X1) is not implemented; its measurements and costs are in F4 and in the evidence archive.

A note on one figure. The "91 of 480" in point 2 is the number the ruling was given. The review that finished afterwards narrowed what it measures, and the report's F2 states it: it is the count of one check (evaluated at the returned point from a fresh residual, with no precision floor), 89 of the 91 are within the target and 2 undecided, and only 9 of the 91 had a stopping pass that changed nothing. The per-visit statistic, a check restricted to empty passes and a precision floor were not measured. The instruction of point 2 stands as written; the research issue starts from the corrected description.
